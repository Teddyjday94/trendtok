Privacy policy

TrendTok is a personal tool used by a single account owner to analyze their own TikTok account and public TikTok trends.

What it collects

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives their profile details (name, username, avatar, follower and like counts) and a list of their public videos with view, like, comment and share counts. It doesn't receive passwords, private messages or private videos.

How it is used

The data is used only to show the owner charts and suggestions about their own account. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in the owner's own database. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Choosing “Disconnect TikTok” in the app revokes access and deletes the stored account data. Access can also be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services.
